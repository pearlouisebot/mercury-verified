Founder note

Founder-provided — not verified
This demo is intentionally opinionated about trust: the bank should own the signed factual substrate, while the founder supplies context and narrative separately. That makes the page useful to investors, lenders, acquirers, and partners without pretending the founder-hosted UI is the source of truth.

Why this matters

  • Read-only UI controls are not enough if the founder controls the server.
  • A bank-signed snapshot is stronger than a founder-managed company profile.
  • Mercury is uniquely positioned to separate verified facts from derived interpretation.
  • The product opportunity is not “more dashboard.” It is trust, provenance, and safe AI-assisted financial workflows.
